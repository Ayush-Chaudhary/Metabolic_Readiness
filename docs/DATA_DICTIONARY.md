## 1. Feature Summary

We are building a Natural Language Query ("NLQ") / "Why's" feature for Simon, the chatbot. When a user asks *why* their **Weight** or **Glucose** is trending in a certain direction, Simon must:

1. Evaluate the **"What"** (outcome trend: Weight or Glucose) over a **rolling 14-day window**, with specific data-sufficiency gates (see §6).
2. Evaluate every eligible **"Why"** (habit: food, activity, sleep, mood, anxiety, BP, medication) over the same window.
3. Compare each habit against **correlation thresholds** and **opportunity scales** (green / yellow / red) that vary by the user's **gender**, **diabetes type**, and **A1C target**.
4. Run a **Medication Check** (highest-priority "Why") whenever the user has a qualified weight- or glucose-management med in their list.
5. Detect **hypoglycemic trends** (separate conversational scenario).
6. Rank the "Why's" by priority × magnitude-of-change and return a ranked, grouped response.

## 7. Appendix — Data Sufficiency Gates (for reference)

Taken verbatim from `NLQ - why's - Time and Data Requirements.csv`. All windows are **14 days**.

| Category | Sub-category | Minimum data requirement |
|---|---|---|
| Glucose — CGM | TIR | 80 % active sensor time |
| Glucose — Manual BG | Per-day reading | 1 reading/day for ≥ 10 of 14 days |
| Weight | Weight | ≥ 2 logged weights per week |
| Diet — all nutrients & food groups | — | 10 logged days of entries |
| Activity | Steps | 10 days system-tracked steps |
| Activity | Total time | no minimum |
| Sleep | Duration / Rating | 10 logged days / 10 logged ratings |
| Mood | Rating | 10 logged days, ≥ 1 rating/day |
| Anxiety | Rating | 10 logged days, ≥ 1 rating/day |
| Blood Pressure | BP | 8 individual readings in 14 days |
| Medication | Med list | User must have a weight- or glucose-specific med on file → triggers "Are you taking X as prescribed?" Med-Check |
